Choose Wisely

Beginner Tips for New Adventurers

Save Often

F5 for quick save. Choices matter. You can always reload if things go wrong.

Explore Everything

Talk to everyone, read books, check containers. XP and items everywhere.

Long Rest Frequently

Camp events advance companion stories. Don't rush. Rest after major fights.

Use Environment

Throw enemies off cliffs, use height advantage, interact with barrels and chandeliers.

Essential Early Game Tips

Loot Everything

Sell to vendors. Camp supplies for resting.

Steal Wisely

Use turn-based mode for theft. Astarion is best.

Talk to Animals

Speak with Animals spell unlocks quests.

Shove is Free

Bonus action shove can save lives.

Combat Tactics

Advantage is Key

Guiding Bolt, Faerie Fire, or height for advantage. Sneak Attack requires it.

Action Economy

More actions win fights. Haste, Action Surge, and bonus actions matter.

Crowd Control

Sleep, Hold Person, Command. Prevent enemies from acting.

Height Advantage

Higher ground = +2 attack rolls. Use jumping and flying.

Surface Interactions

Fire + Grease = Burning

Grease spell + Firebolt = burning surface. Extra damage.

Water + Lightning = Electrified

Create Water + Lightning Bolt = AoE stun.

Act-by-Act Walkthrough

Act 1: Wilderness

Levels 1-5. Explore crash site, Grove, Goblin Camp, Underdark, Mountain Pass.

30-40 hours

Act 2: Shadow-Cursed Lands

Levels 5-8. Last Light Inn, Moonrise Towers, Gauntlet of Shar.

20-30 hours

Act 3: Baldur's Gate

Levels 8-12. Lower City, Wyrm's Rock, House of Hope, final battle.

30-40 hours

Act 1 Critical Path

Nautiloid Crash

Save Shadowheart, get Us (intellect devourer), loot all. Use transponder.

Druid Grove

Talk to Zevlor, Kagha. Decide: save grove or side with goblins.

Goblin Camp

Kill leaders: Priestess Gut, Dror Ragzlin, Minthara. Save Halsin.

Mountain Pass or Underdark

Choose path to Act 2. Both have content. Do both before proceeding.

Act 2 Critical Path

Last Light Inn

Save Isobel, meet Jaheira. Protect inn from attack.

Moonrise Towers

Infiltrate, rescue prisoners, find Ketheric's relic.

Gauntlet of Shar

Shadowheart quest. Trials, Nightsong choice - major story impact.
