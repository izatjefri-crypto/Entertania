Advanced Tips & Secrets

Money Making Strategies

Bulk Buying

Buy in bulk from wholesaler. Lower cost per unit, higher profit margin.

Dynamic Pricing

Raise prices on weekends. Lower on weekdays. Match demand.

Private Label

Unlock store brand products. Higher margins, loyal customers.

Seasonal Displays

Holiday items = high profit. Stock up before holidays.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Over-expanding Too Fast

Don't take loans for big expansions. Grow organically. Debt kills profits.

Ignoring Shoplifters

Security cameras pay for themselves. Theft adds up fast.

Poor Shelf Placement

Put high-margin items at eye level. Kids' items low, adult items high.

Neglecting Online Orders

Delivery and pickup services = extra revenue stream.

Maximizing Customer Satisfaction

Cleanliness

Sweep floors, clean windows. Clean stores attract more customers.

Music

Background music improves shopping experience. Keep it moderate.

Samples

Free samples increase sales of promoted items.

Secret Tips

• Loyalty program unlocks at Level 12 - sign up customers for recurring revenue

• Rainy days = more customers - stock up before forecast

• Near-expiry items - mark down 30%, sell fast

• Employee of the month - morale boost for minimal cost

• Hidden suppliers - unlock by reaching reputation milestones
