Getting Started

The Van der Linde Gang

Arthur Morgan

The protagonist. Dutch's most trusted enforcer. Tough, loyal, and starting to question the gang's direction.

🔫 Main Protagonist

Dutch van der Linde

The charismatic leader of the gang. Idealistic but increasingly unstable as the story progresses.

🎩 Gang Leader

John Marston

A former gang member trying to leave the life. Father figure to Jack. Protagonist of RDR1.

🐺 Family Man

Sadie Adler

Widow turned bounty hunter. One of the toughest members of the gang. Fearsome fighter.

🤠 Bounty Hunter

Core Mechanics

Dead Eye

Slows time to mark and shoot multiple targets. Levels up as you progress.

Honor System

Your actions affect honor. High honor gives discounts, low honor unlocks different story moments.

Health & Stamina

Improve by doing activities. Health from fighting, stamina from running/swimming, dead eye from hunting.

Grooming

Keep Arthur clean. Hair and beard grow over time. Visit barbers for styling.

Essential Early Tips

Explore

The world is huge. Take time to explore, hunt, and find hidden locations. Main quests will always be there.

Make Money

Sell perfect pelts to trappers, loot enemies, do stranger missions. Rob trains and stores for quick cash.

Honor Matters

High honor gives discounts in stores. Low honor unlocks different story options. Choose your path.

Bond with Horse

Lead, feed, and brush your horse. Higher bond gives better handling and special moves.

Chapter 1: Colter (Prologue)

The gang is trapped in a blizzard in the mountains. Follow the tutorial missions to learn basic controls. Save after the bear attack mission to create a manual save point.

1-2 hours 5 missions

Story & Chapters

Chapter 2: Horseshoe Overlook

The gang settles near Valentine. Best time to explore, hunt, and upgrade camp. Do as many side activities as possible before progressing.

Heartlands 15+ missions

Chapter 3: Clemens Point

Camp near Rhodes. Conflict between the Braithwaites and Grays intensifies. Start investing in satchels from Pearson.

Scarlett Meadows 14 missions

Chapter 4: Shady Belle

The gang moves to a plantation in the swamps near Saint Denis. Major story developments. Save before important choices.

Bayou Nwa 13 missions

Chapter 5: Guarma

The gang is shipwrecked on a tropical island. Linear chapter with unique weapons and enemies. All weapons and items are temporarily unavailable.

Guarma 6 missions

Chapter 6: Beaver Hollow

Final chapter with Arthur. Moral choices here affect the ending. Complete all stranger missions and companion requests before progressing.

Roanoke Ridge 14 missions

Epilogue: Pronghorn Ranch & Beecher's Hope

Play as John Marston. Build a ranch, reunite with old friends, and set up for the first Red Dead Redemption. New Austin becomes accessible.

West Elizabeth 12 missions

Hunting & Fishing Guide

Perfect Pelts Guide

To get perfect pelts, you need the right weapon for each animal size and a 3-star rating.

Animal Size Weapon Examples
Small Varmint Rifle Rabbits, raccoons, badgers, muskrats
Medium Repeater/Rifle Foxes, coyotes, deer, pronghorn
Large Rifle (headshot) Elk, moose, bears, bison
Birds Small game arrows Songbirds, woodpeckers, owls

Fishing Tips

Best Spots

Dakota River for salmon, Owanjila for trout, Lagras for catfish, San Luis for sturgeon.

Lures vs Bait

Special lures catch legendary fish. Bread for small fish, cheese for medium, corn for large.

Time of Day

Most fish bite better at dawn and dusk. Check compendium for specific preferences.

Reeling Tips

Pull rod up when fish struggles, reel when it's tired. Don't reel while fish is fighting.

Tips & Secrets

Money Making Guide

Treasure Maps

Find treasure maps from strangers or random encounters. Each map leads to gold bars worth $500.

Gold Bars

Find gold bars at Limpany, Braithwaite Manor, and train wrecks. Each sells for $500 at fences.

Perfect Pelts

Sell perfect pelts to trappers. Legendary pelts unlock special clothing items.

Rob Trains

Rob trains in tunnels to avoid lawmen. Loot passengers and safes.

Missable Content

Companion Requests: Mary-Beth, Tilly, and others have requests that disappear after Chapter 4.
Mary Linton Missions: Arthur's ex-fiancée. Complete all in Chapter 2-4.
Money Lending Missions: Strauss missions affect honor. Complete before Chapter 6.
Camp Requests: Pearson's rabbit request, Dutch's pipe request - do them early!

⚠️ CHAPTER 6 SPOILER: Arthur's health declines in Chapter 6. Complete all side missions and collectibles before the last few story missions. Some become unavailable after Chapter 6.
