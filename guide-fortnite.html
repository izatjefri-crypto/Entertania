Getting Started

The Basics

Fortnite is a free-to-play battle royale game where 100 players fight to be the last one standing. You can play solo, duo, or squad. The game combines shooting mechanics with unique building elements that set it apart from other battle royales.

Landing Strategies

Named Locations

High loot, high competition. Great for aggressive players who want early fights.

Unnamed Spots

Lower loot, safer landing. Perfect for beginners learning the game.

Hot Drops

Popular areas with multiple players. Good for practicing combat skills.

Stealth Approach

Land away from the bus path to avoid early encounters and loot safely.

Loot Tiers

Common (Grey)

Basic weapons. Low damage. Found everywhere.

Uncommon (Green)

Slightly better stats. Common in chests.

Rare (Blue)

Good weapons. Found in chests and floor loot.

Epic (Purple)

Strong weapons. Rare drops from chests.

Legendary (Gold)

Best weapons. Supply drops and bosses.

Mythic (Red)

Special boss weapons. Extremely powerful.

Essential First Steps

1. Complete the tutorial to learn basic controls
2. Play Team Rumble to practice without elimination pressure
3. Learn to build simple structures (walls, ramps)
4. Land at less popular spots first
5. Use visual audio settings for better awareness

Building & Editing Guide

Basic Builds

Wall

Basic defense. Place in front of you for cover.

Ramp

Gain height advantage. Push enemies.

Floor

Create platforms. Bridge gaps.

Cone

Protect from above. Block enemy builds.

Essential Techniques

90s

Quickly gain height by building 3 walls and 3 ramps in a rotation. Practice in creative mode.

Box Up

When getting shot, build a 1x1 box for protection. Add a ramp for cover.

Side Jump

Jump to the side while placing a ramp to maintain momentum and height.

Thwifo Cone

Advanced technique. Place a cone, ramp, and floor for quick height gain.

Editing Basics

Window Edit: Edit a wall to create a window for shooting.
Door Edit: Quick exit from your box.
Ramp Edit: Edit ramp to peak over and shoot.
Cone Edit: Edit cone to create a ramp or peek hole.

💡 PRO TIP: Practice editing in creative mode for 10-15 minutes before playing. Master simple edits before moving to advanced techniques.

Game Modes Guide

Battle Royale Modes

Solo

100 players, last one standing. Pure skill test.

Duos

Play with a friend. Teamwork required.

Trios

3-player squads. Balanced fights.

Squads

4-player teams. Most popular mode.

Zero Build Modes

Same as BR but without building. Focus on shooting, movement, and using environment for cover. Great for players who prefer traditional shooters.

Creative Modes

Box Fights

Close-quarters practice. Best for aim training.

Zone Wars

End-game simulation. Practice rotations.

Edit Courses

Practice editing techniques. Build muscle memory.

Special Modes

Team Rumble: Respawn mode. Great for warmup and quests.
Reload: Limited-time mode with unique mechanics.
LEGO Fortnite: Survival/crafting mode.
Rocket Racing: Arcade racing mode.

Tips & Strategies

Beginner Tips

Visual Audio

Turn on Visual Audio in settings. Shows footsteps and gunshots direction.

Keybinds

Optimize your keybinds. Use close keys for building pieces.

Mouse Sensitivity

Find a comfortable sensitivity. Lower for accuracy, higher for building.

Gear Up

Always carry at least 2 healing items. AR, Shotgun, SMG is standard loadout.

Advanced Strategies

Piece Control

Place structures to trap and control enemy movement. Build walls around opponents.

High Ground

Always maintain height advantage. Easier to shoot, harder to be shot.

Rotations

Plan your movement with the storm. Stay ahead of the circle. Use vehicles for quick rotations.

Battle Pass Tips

• Complete weekly quests for XP
• Play Team Rumble for easy challenges
• Save VBucks for next battle pass
• Milestone quests give huge XP
