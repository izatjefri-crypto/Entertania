Getting Started

Before You Begin

From the start, you can choose to play Ghost of Tsushima in English, or in Japanese with English subtitles for a more authentic samurai cinema-style experience. You'll also have the option to play in Kurosawa Mode, inspired by the legendary filmmaker, which changes the world to black and white, cranks up the wind, and makes subtle changes to visuals and sound. You can enable this option at any time in the menu.

Let the Island Guide You

Follow the Wind

Watch your surroundings for clues like smoke on the horizon or mysterious trees. Call the Guiding Wind to help navigate towards your current objective. You can pick any point on the map and follow the wind to find it.

Animal Guides

Pay attention to animals like golden birds or foxes, which can lead you to special landmarks or activities. Fox dens lead to Inari shrines for charm slots.

Musical Moments

Try climbing as high as you can and getting a good look at the island, then play Jin's flute. A break for a nice song may have unexpected effects.

Explore the Map

While you'll need to explore to find new parts of Tsushima initially, once you've discovered an area, you'll be able to return to it with Fast Travel.

Essential Early Upgrades

Perfect Healing Parry

Helps offset early misses as you learn to time your blocks and attacks.

Deflect Arrows

Helps you avoid threats from afar while taking on closer enemies.

Iron Will

Spend two points of your Resolve meter after defeat to jump back into the fight.

First Things To Do

1. Acquire Traveler's Attire

Visit any merchant (like in Hiyoshi Springs) to get the Traveler's Attire for free. While wearing it, the map uncovers in a larger area, and the controller vibrates near artifacts.

2. Get Charm of Okuninushi

Visit Mending Rock Shrine (east of starting position). This charm heals you while not in combat, making the game much easier.

3. Visit Golden Temple

Located in central Izuhara, this serves as your base of operations with a Gift Altar, Bowyer, Swordsmith, and Armorer all in one place.

4. Get Charm of Inari

Find Arrow Peak Shrine (south of Golden Temple). This charm doubles resources gained and increases enemy loot drops.

Combat & Stances Guide

The Four Combat Stances

Each stance helps you eliminate certain types of opponents. You can switch between them on the fly by holding R2. Unlock new stances by observing and killing enemy leaders.

Stone Stance

Best for attacking enemies that use swords and katanas. Starting stance. Effective against swordsmen.

vs Swordsmen

Water Stance

Best for attacking enemies that use shields. Breaks through shield defense with surging strikes.

vs Shieldmen

Wind Stance

Best for attacking enemies that use spears. Includes sweeping kicks that can interrupt spear attacks.

vs Spearmen

Moon Stance

Best for strong, larger enemies (Brutes). Features heavy punishing strikes that can launch enemies.

vs Brutes

Combat Mechanics

Blocking & Parrying

Hold L1 to block most attacks. Tap L1 just before an enemy strike to perform a parry, allowing a brutal counterattack. Perfect Parry unlocks stronger counters.

Dodging

Press Circle to dodge. Jin cannot block red attacks (unblockable) - you must dodge or roll. Perfect Dodge slows time for a follow-up attack.

Standoff

Press Up on D-pad before enemies spot you. Hold Triangle and release when the enemy attacks. With upgrades, you can chain up to 3 kills. Sakai Armor extends this further.

Ghost Stance

Unlocked in Act 2. Get 5 kills without taking damage (or 7 normally) to enter Ghost Stance, allowing instant kills on multiple enemies. Combine with Sakai Armor for 5 standoff kills, then switch to Ghost Armor.

Ranged Combat

Short Bow

Best suited for rapid shots. Good for unarmored enemies.

Long Bow

Good for attacking armored enemies. Higher chance to kill with headshots.

Concentration

Press L3 to activate slow-motion while aiming, making it easier to hit moving targets.

Armor & Gear Guide

Essential Armor Sets

Each armor offers unique stats and advantages. Consider the right attire for any given moment. You can upgrade armor to strengthen effects or purchase dyes to change appearance.

Traveler's Attire

Uncover the map in a larger area. Controller vibrates near artifacts. Set Guiding Wind to find collectibles.

+ Exploration

Samurai Clan Armor

Reduces damage, increases health, restores more resolve. Good early-game combat armor.

+ Defense + Health

Sakai Clan Armor

Increases melee damage, more health, extends standoff kills. Fully upgraded allows 5 standoff kills.

+ Melee Damage + Standoff

Ghost Armor

Reduces Ghost Stance requirement from 7 to 5 kills without damage. Terrifies nearby enemies on assassination.

+ Ghost Stance + Terror

Tadayori's Armor

Combined with Longbow and right charms, this is the most broken thing in the game. Spam longbow heavy arrows from safe distance.

+ Bow Damage

Gosaku's Armor

Great for duels or open combat. Increases stagger damage.

+ Stagger Damage

Ghost Weapons

Kunai

Stun opponents, reducing need for stance advantage. Upgraded throws multiple daggers.

Powder Bombs

Can weaken, stun, or kill enemies. Best for stationary targets.

Smoke Bombs

Blind enemies, allowing escapes or chain assassinations.

Sticky Bomb

Excellent for weakening and stunning Brutes.

Charms to Prioritize

Charm of Okuninushi

Heals you while not in combat. Start every encounter fully healed.

Charm of Inari

Doubles resources gained from all sources. Essential for upgrades.

Enemy Guide

Enemy Difficulty Tiers

Enemies appear in different colors indicating difficulty and equipment level. The frequency changes as you progress through chapters.

Grey Tier

Basic enemies with simple patterns. Appears only in prologue and Chapter 1.

Red Tier

Light armored. Higher stun resistance, throw kunai at distance. Appears from mid Chapter 1.

Blue Tier

Medium armored. Helmet prevents headshots. May shoot bows in succession.

Yellow Tier

Heavily armored. Use fire weapons, higher damage. Appears in Chapter 2.

Purple Tier

Elite troops. Highest damage and stun resistance. Iki Island only.

Enemy Types & Counters

Swordsmen

Basic sword users. Dual-wield variants have very short attack windows - requires consecutive parries. Greatsword users have slow but sweeping attacks.

Stone Stance

Shieldmen

Carry shields that block arrows from front. Thorn shields have shield-bash attacks. Fire swords can light Jin on fire.

Water Stance

Spearmen

Long attack range. Elite spearmen have very fast thrusts and carry small shields to block headshots.

Wind Stance

Brutes

Large enemies with heavy weapons. Mace, Shield, Ax, and Fire Spear variants. Fire Spear has devastating wide area fire attack.

Moon Stance

Archers

Call out before firing. Elite archers shoot 2-3 arrows. Fire arrows set you ablaze. Poison arrows (late game) blur vision.

Deflect Arrows

Engineers

Throw bombs from distance. Flash bombs blind entire screen. Prioritize elimination.

Kunai

Animal Enemies

Dogs and bears use red attacks frequently. Try to eliminate them before they get close. After dodging, attack once and prepare to dodge again.

Mythic Tales Guide

The Curse of Uchitsune

Unlock the Longbow. Complete the archery challenges and defeat the tengu demon.

Reward: Longbow

The Legend of Tadayori

Honor the legendary archer. Complete horseback archery challenges.

Reward: Tadayori's Armor

The Heavenly Strike

Learn the legendary technique from a master swordsman. Defeat six Kojiro students.

Reward: Heavenly Strike

The Six Blades of Kojiro

Track down and defeat six master swordsmen across Tsushima.

Reward: Kensei Armor

The Flaming Blade of Kojiro

Unlock the power of fire. Complete platforming challenges and defeat the fire spirit.

Reward: Dance of Wrath

The Undying Flame

Channel the power of fire into your sword. Defeat the fire spirit in the cursed temple.

Reward: Way of the Flame

Dance of Wrath

This Mythic Technique allows Jin to strike three enemies in quick succession. Extremely powerful when surrounded. Can be upgraded to strike additional enemies.

Iki Island DLC Guide

How to Start Iki Island

Complete Act 1 of the main story to unlock the "Journey into the Past" tale. Track it in the "Tales of Iki" section. Investigate the settlement at Drowned Man's Shore in southern Toyotama. After the cutscene and combat, speak to the boat captain to sail to Iki.

⚠️ IMPORTANT: You will be unable to return to Tsushima until completing the Iki Island main story. Complete any unfinished business before departing.

Forbidden Shrines (PlayStation Easter Eggs)

The Director's Cut includes three special shrines honoring PlayStation classics. Each requires specific equipment and actions to unlock exclusive cosmetic rewards.

Forbidden Shrine

Location: Senjo Gorge (Southwest Iki). Red leaves, dark atmosphere.

Solution: Wear Kensei Armor, take damage, heal (D-pad down), stand still.

Reward: Yharnam Helm + Vestments (Bloodborne)

Shrine of Ash

Location: Eastern Iki, north of Tatsu's Ladder.

Solution: Wear Sakai Clan Armor, perform heavy attack (Triangle) near statue.

Reward: Spartan Helm + Ghost of Sparta Vestments (God of War)

Shrine in Shadow

Location: North-central Iki, southwest of Sakai Fortress.

Solution: Wear Ghost Armor, crouch and slowly approach statue, remain still.

Reward: Mask of the Wanderer + Colossus Vestments (Shadow of the Colossus)

Iki Island Tips

Higher Difficulty

Iki Island enemies are significantly tougher. Purple-tier enemies have unique abilities and higher damage. Recommended level: After completing main story or at least Act 3.

New Horse

If your horse didn't survive the shipwreck, you'll find a new companion on Iki.

Sarugami Armor

Found on Iki. Greatly increases perfect parry and perfect dodge windows. Excellent for duels.

Horse Charge

New technique allows charging through enemies on horseback.
