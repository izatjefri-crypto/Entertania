First-Time Player Tips

Last Hitting

Practice last-hitting minions for gold. Core mechanic.

Minimap Awareness

Glance every few seconds. Track enemy roams.

Summoner Spells

Flash is essential. Take Ignite/Teleport based on role.

Warding

Buy Control Wards. Place stealth wards in river.

Beginner-Friendly Champions

Garen (Top)

Simple kit, sustain, no mana. Great to learn trading and split pushing.

Annie (Mid)

Easy last-hitting with Q, point-and-click stun, high burst.

Ashe (ADC)

Built-in slow, global vision with E, good kiting practice.

Soraka (Support)

Healing focused, safe laning, global ult impact.

Roles & Current Meta (Patch 25.5)

Top Lane (Island)

Bruisers, tanks, duelists. Focus on split pushing and teleport plays. Strong picks: K'Sante, Jax, Malphite.

Sustain Tank or Carry

Jungle (The Chessmaster)

Controls objectives, ganks lanes. Pathing and smite crucial. Meta: Lee Sin, Viego, Maokai.

Objective control Early ganks

Mid Lane (Playmaker)

Mages or assassins. Roam potential. Strong: Ahri, Sylas, Taliyah.

Burst Roaming

Bot Lane (ADC)

Attack damage carry. Scale into late game. Meta: Jinx, Kai'Sa, Ezreal.

Positioning Crit/On-Hit

Support (The Visionary)

Peel, engage, or poke. Roam with jungler. Top: Thresh, Nautilus, Lulu.

Vision Peel/Engage

Essential Combos (Example: Ahri)

Charm -> Orb -> Foxfire

Land E (Charm), then immediately Q (Orb) and W (Foxfire) for guaranteed true damage.

Lee Sin "Insec"

Q -> Q -> Ward -> W -> R. Kick enemy into your team.

Armor vs MR

Advanced Tips & Micro/Macro

Animation Canceling

Auto between abilities (Riven, Renekton). Practice in Practice Tool.

Cooldown Tracking

Mentally note enemy Flash (5 min) and ult timers.

F-keys

Use F1-F5 to quickly check teammates' lanes.

Baiting

Fake recall in brush, then punish overconfident enemies.

Wave Management

Freezing, Slow pushing, Fast pushing

Freeze near your tower for safety. Slow push to stack wave then dive. Fast push to reset or roam.
