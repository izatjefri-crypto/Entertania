Marvel Tōkon: Fighting Souls Gameplay

Official gameplay showcase

Marvel Tōkon: Fighting Souls is a 2.5D fighting game where players compete in intense tag-team battles using an ensemble of Marvel Comics characters. The objective is to defeat all opponents by draining their shared health bar through strategic attacks and combos.

The game features a unique 4v4 system that allows players to control one main character alongside three assist fighters. Players can switch between characters during combat using an active tag system, enabling dynamic team-based strategies. At the start of each match, only the main fighter and one assist are available, with additional characters unlocked through in-game conditions such as Wall Breaks.

Unlike traditional tag fighters, all team members share a single Vital Gauge that expands as more fighters become available. Assist mechanics are governed by the Assemble Gauge, allowing players to summon support attacks and perform Crossovers to seamlessly transition between characters during combat.

The combat system includes Light, Medium, and Heavy attacks, along with Unique character-specific abilities such as Captain America’s shield throws and Iron Man’s repulsor blasts. A Skill Gauge enables powerful EX moves and cinematic Ultimate Skills, which can be chained together for devastating team combos.

Inspired by previous Arc System Works titles, Fighting Souls features visually rich multi-area stages drawn from the Marvel Universe. These stages transition dynamically during battles through Wall Break mechanics, creating an immersive and cinematic fighting experience.
