Red Dead Redemption 2 Review

Reviewed: October 26, 2018
By MICHAEL HIGHSAM
Posted: 2 weeks ago
13 comments
10 Masterpiece

A masterpiece of storytelling and open-world design

A stunning achievement in open-world gaming, Red Dead Redemption 2 sets a new standard for storytelling, immersion, and attention to detail. Rockstar Games has crafted a living, breathing world that demands to be explored.

— Michael Highsam

Introduction

Red Dead Redemption 2 is more than just a game; it's a living, breathing world that stands as one of the most ambitious and accomplished interactive experiences ever created. Rockstar Games has not only crafted a prequel to their 2010 classic but has elevated the entire medium with an unprecedented level of detail, storytelling depth, and immersive gameplay.

Set in 1899 at the end of the Wild West era, you play as Arthur Morgan, a senior member of the Van der Linde gang. As outlaws on the run from law enforcement and rival gangs, you'll experience the slow death of the cowboy lifestyle while forging your own path through a massive, reactive world.

"Red Dead Redemption 2 isn't just a game about the Wild West - it's a game about loyalty, mortality, and the inevitable march of progress. It's a masterpiece that will be studied for years to come."

The Living World

The world of Red Dead Redemption 2 is arguably its greatest achievement. Every forest, swamp, mountain town, and dusty saloon feels authentic and alive. Animals hunt and are hunted, weather patterns shift dynamically, and NPCs go about their daily routines with remarkable believability. You can spend hours just hunting, fishing, exploring abandoned cabins, or helping strangers you meet on the road.

The attention to detail is staggering. Your horse's testicles shrink in cold weather. Gun oil actually degrades weapon performance. NPCs remember how you treated them and react accordingly. These details aren't just gimmicks; they build a world that feels real and responsive to your actions.

Story and Characters

At its heart, Red Dead Redemption 2 is a character study of Arthur Morgan and the Van der Linde gang. Arthur is one of gaming's most compelling protagonists - a tough, loyal outlaw whose internal conflict between his brutal lifestyle and his desire for redemption drives the narrative. The supporting cast is equally memorable, from the idealistic Dutch van der Linde to the hot-headed Bill Williamson and the tragic Sadie Adler.

The story spans six chapters and an epilogue, each building toward an emotional conclusion that rivals the best of cinema. Rockstar's writing has never been sharper, balancing moments of high tension with quiet character moments and genuine humor.

Gameplay Depth

The gameplay systems are equally impressive. The Dead Eye targeting system returns with new depth, allowing you to paint targets in slow motion with increasing precision as you progress. Gunplay feels weighty and impactful, with each weapon having distinct handling characteristics.

Beyond combat, you'll manage your camp, contribute supplies, upgrade equipment, and bond with gang members. Hunting and fishing provide resources for crafting and upgrades, while a robust honor system tracks your moral choices, affecting how NPCs react to you and even altering key story moments.

Pros and Cons

Pros

  • Unparalleled open world detail
  • Deep, emotional storytelling
  • Arthur Morgan is a legendary protagonist
  • Endless activities and exploration
  • Gorgeous visuals and soundtrack
  • Red Dead Online adds endless replayability

Cons

  • Deliberate pacing may feel slow
  • Controls can feel cumbersome
  • Wanted system can be frustrating
  • Long intro section before open world opens up

Visual and Audio Design

Running on Rockstar's proprietary RAGE engine, Red Dead Redemption 2 is one of the best-looking games ever made. From the snow-capped mountains of Ambarino to the swamps of Lemoyne, every region has a distinct visual identity. Lighting, weather effects, and character animations are state-of-the-art.

The soundtrack by Woody Jackson is a masterpiece in its own right, blending original compositions with licensed period music that perfectly captures the game's tone. The voice acting is exceptional across the board, with Roger Clark's performance as Arthur Morgan deserving particular praise.

Verdict

Red Dead Redemption 2 is not just a game - it's an experience that will stay with you long after the credits roll. It's a meditation on loyalty, mortality, and the end of an era, wrapped in one of the most detailed and immersive open worlds ever created. For anyone who appreciates storytelling, atmosphere, and craftsmanship in games, this is essential. A true masterpiece and an easy 10/10.
