Agents Overview

Valorant agents are divided into four roles: Duelists (entry fraggers), Controllers (smoke specialists), Initiators (info gatherers), and Sentinels (defenders).

    Economy Tips

    • Win bonus: +3000 credits for winning

    • Loss bonus: +1900 credits (increases each consecutive loss)

    • Plant spike: +300 credits for your team

    • Kill bonus: +200 credits per kill

    Game Mechanics

    Spray Patterns

    Each weapon has a unique recoil pattern. Practice controlling the spray in the range.

    Movement Mechanics

    Counter-Strafing

    Tap opposite direction key to stop faster and shoot accurately.

    Bunny Hopping

    Jump while strafing to maintain momentum.

    Ability Interactions

    Ability Type Description Counter
    Flashes Blind enemies temporarily Turn away, cover eyes
    Smokes Block vision Scan abilities, push through
    Molotovs Area denial Move away, heal through

    Tips & Strategies

    Crosshair Placement

    Always aim at head level where enemies will appear. Never aim at the ground.

    Map Awareness

    Check minimap constantly. Call out enemy positions immediately.

    Communication

    Use voice chat and ping system. Give clear callouts.

    Utility Usage

    Don't waste utility. Use abilities to gather info or take space.

    Ranked Tips

    Climbing the Ladder

    • Focus on 2-3 agents max
    • Warm up in deathmatch
    • Review your losses
    • Play with consistent teammates
