Resident Evil Requiem Review

Reviewed: March 15, 2026
By TRISTAN OGILVIE
Posted: 1 day ago
10 comments
8.5 Great

Requiem for a scream

The latest installment in the survival horror franchise brings back the tension and terror that fans have been craving.

— Tristan Ogilvie

Introduction

Resident Evil Requiem marks a bold return to the survival horror roots that made the franchise famous, while introducing new mechanics that breathe fresh life into the series. Set between the events of Resident Evil 7 and Village, this latest installment follows FBI analyst Grace Ashcroft as she investigates a mysterious outbreak in a remote Eastern European village.

The game opens with a chilling atmosphere that immediately sets the tone. The developers have masterfully crafted an environment that feels both familiar and terrifyingly new. Every shadow hides a threat, every sound could be your last.

"Resident Evil Requiem doesn't just rely on jump scares; it builds tension through masterful pacing and environmental storytelling that would make even the original directors proud."

Gameplay Evolution

The core gameplay mechanics have been refined to offer a perfect balance between action and horror. The inventory system returns with some clever updates, and the new "Fear System" dynamically adjusts enemy behavior based on your playstyle. Play aggressively, and enemies become more cunning. Play stealthily, and they'll start hunting in packs.

Leon S. Kennedy makes a welcome return, and his sections offer a more action-oriented approach that contrasts beautifully with Grace's survival-focused gameplay. The dual-protagonist structure works exceptionally well, offering two distinct perspectives on the same terrifying events.

Visual and Audio Design

The RE Engine continues to impress, delivering some of the most photorealistic horror visuals to date. Character models are incredibly detailed, and the lighting system creates an oppressive atmosphere that never lets up. The sound design deserves special mention - every creak, every distant moan, every sudden silence is carefully crafted to keep you on edge.

Pros and Cons

Pros

  • Tense, atmospheric horror
  • Excellent dual-protagonist system
  • Stunning visuals and sound design
  • Meaningful player choices
  • Great replay value

Cons

  • Some pacing issues in middle chapters
  • Puzzle difficulty inconsistent
  • Limited enemy variety

Story and Characters

Without spoiling anything, the narrative takes some bold risks that pay off spectacularly. Grace Ashcroft is a welcome addition to the Resident Evil universe - intelligent, capable, and genuinely terrified in a way that makes her relatable. Leon's return is handled with care, giving fans exactly what they want while advancing his character arc.

The story connects dots between previous games in satisfying ways, answering questions fans have had for years while raising new mysteries for future installments.

Verdict

Resident Evil Requiem is a triumphant return to form that honors the series' legacy while pushing it forward. With its tense atmosphere, smart gameplay innovations, and compelling story, it's an essential experience for horror fans and a strong contender for survival horror game of the year.
