Getting Started

The Three Protagonists

Michael De Santa

Retired bank robber in witness protection. Family man living in Rockford Hills. Special ability: Bullet Time - slows down time while shooting.

Driver • Shooter

Franklin Clinton

Young gang member looking for better opportunities. Works for a repo company. Special ability: Driving Focus - slows time while driving.

Driver • Repo Man

Trevor Philips

Career criminal and Michael's old partner. Runs a drug empire in Sandy Shores. Special ability: Rage Mode - deals double damage.

Pilot • Fighter

Difficulty Levels

Easy

Enemies deal less damage, aim worse. Players have more health. Recommended for story-focused players.

Normal

Balanced difficulty. Standard enemy behavior. Recommended for first playthrough.

Hard

Enemies deal more damage, aim better. Better loot drops. Unlocks after completing the game.

Essential Early Tips

Stock Market Tips

Complete the assassination missions for Lester AFTER finishing the main story to maximize profits. Invest all money before each mission.

Save Often

Sleep in your bed to save the game. Always save before starting missions to avoid losing progress.

Max Stats

Play tennis, golf, and darts to increase strength. Take taxi rides to learn the map faster.

Hidden Packages

Collect all 50 spaceship parts and 50 letter scraps for unique vehicles and rewards.

Story Mode Walkthrough

Prologue: North Yankton

Tutorial mission with Michael, Trevor, and Brad. Follow the truck, shoot enemies, and escape the police. Sets up the story for the trio.

15 min $20,000

Franklin & Lamar

First mission as Franklin. Repossession job gone wrong. Learn basic driving and shooting mechanics.

10 min $2,000

Repossession

Franklin's first mission with Simeon. Steal a motorcycle from the Grove Street Families. First encounter with Ballas.

15 min $1,500

Complications

Michael meets Franklin after Franklin tries to repossess his son's car. Chase Jimmy on a stolen bike and retrieve the car.

20 min $2,500

Marriage Counseling

Michael chases his wife's tennis coach across the city. Trevor's first appearance in Los Santos. High-speed chase with a tow truck.

25 min $5,000

Character Switching

Press Down on D-pad to switch between characters. Use this during missions to coordinate attacks. Each character has unique abilities and contacts.

Steal a super weapon from Merryweather. Choose Offshore or Deck approach.

$10M+ potential

Rob the Paleto Bay Bank. Military-style assault with heavy weapons.

$20M+ potential
Crew: 4 • Setup Cost: $45,000

The Big Score

The final heist. Choose Obvious (loud) or Subtle (stealth) approach.

$200M+ potential
Crew: 6 • Setup Cost: $200,000

Crew Selection Guide

Driver

Choose Taliana for lower cut (5%) and good skill. Karim is cheap but less reliable.

Gunner

Chef (Trevor's friend) is best for heavy missions. Packie is good for stealth.

Hacker

Rickie is cheapest but worst. Paige is best for time-critical heists.

Collectibles Guide

Spaceship Parts

50 parts scattered across the map. Collect all for Omega's mission reward.

50 total Reward: Space Docker vehicle

Letter Scraps

50 scraps forming a map to murder evidence. Collect for Leonora Johnson's mission.

50 total Reward: $100,000 + Hatchet

Nuclear Waste

30 barrels underwater. Requires submarine from Merryweather heist.

30 total Reward: $250,000

Submarine Parts

30 parts for Abigail's mission. Found underwater near sunken submarines.

30 total Reward: $300,000

Peyote Plants

27 plants that turn you into animals for a limited time.

27 total Reward: Animal transformations

Stunt Jumps

50 unique stunt jumps across the map. Complete for vehicle upgrades.

50 total Reward: Vehicle upgrades

Random Events

There are 57 random events in Story Mode. Completing them unlocks vehicles, weapons, and special encounters. Some are time-sensitive!

GTA Online Guide

Best Ways to Make Money

Cayo Perico Heist

Solo-friendly heist. $1-2M per hour. Requires Kosatka submarine ($2.2M).

Casino Heist

$1-2M per hour. Requires Arcade property. Best with 2-3 players.

Agency Contracts

Dr. Dre contract pays $1M. Agency costs $2M+ but offers passive income.

Nightclub

Best passive income. Requires 5 businesses to maximize. $50k per hour.

Acid Lab

Good solo business. $300k per hour. Requires First Dose missions.

Auto Shop

Client cars and mini-heists. $200-300k per hour.

Essential Properties

Kosatka Sub

$2.2M - Required for Cayo Perico. Best investment.

Agency

$2M+ - Imani Tech, payphone hits, Dr. Dre contract.

Arcade

$1.2M+ - Casino Heist, master control terminal.

Nightclub

$1.2M+ - Passive income, Terrorbyte, Oppressor Mk II.

Beginner Tips

1. Do the First Dose missions for Acid Lab (free vehicle)
2. Save for Kosatka submarine first
3. Join the Discord to find heist crews
4. Daily Objectives pay $30k per day
5. Spin the Casino Wheel daily for prizes
6. Use 2-factor authentication for $500k bonus

⚠️ IMPORTANT: GTA Online has a steep learning curve. Start with contact missions and Gerald's missions before attempting heists. Always play in Invite Only sessions to avoid griefers.
