About Warhammer 40,000

In the grim darkness of the far future, there is only war. Warhammer 40,000 is the iconic tabletop wargame where players command armies of Citadel miniatures in epic sci-fi battles. Set in a gothic dystopian galaxy, humanity fights for survival against alien races, chaos cultists, and ancient horrors.

Choose your faction - from the elite Space Marines and Astra Militarum to the hordes of Orks and Tyranids, or the sinister Chaos Space Marines. Beyond the tabletop, the franchise spans numerous video games including Dawn of War, Space Marine, and Rogue Trader, bringing the brutal 41st millennium to life [citation:1]. The 10th edition of the tabletop game was released in June 2023, streamlining rules for new players while maintaining strategic depth [citation:4][citation:7].
