God of War: Sons of Sparta Review

Reviewed: March 7, 2026
By JARRETT GREEN
Posted: 7 days ago
9 comments
9.0 Amazing

This is, unfortunately, Sparta

A return to form that captures the essence of Kratos while adding new depth. The brutal combat and epic scale fans love, now with improved mechanics.

— Jarrett Green

Introduction

God of War: Sons of Sparta takes us back to the brutal world of Greek mythology, following Kratos as he confronts the demons of his past. This installment serves as both a prequel and a love letter to the original God of War games, delivering the same visceral combat and epic scale that made the series famous.

The story follows Kratos as he seeks answers about his brother Deimos, who was taken by Zeus years ago. This personal journey takes us through familiar yet reimagined locations from Greek mythology, all rendered with modern graphics and gameplay improvements.

"Sons of Sparta doesn't just rely on nostalgia; it builds upon the foundation of the original games to create something that feels both familiar and fresh."

Combat Evolution

The Blades of Chaos are back and better than ever. The combat system has been refined to offer more fluid combos and devastating finishers. New magical abilities add depth to encounters, and the return of the rage meter gives players a powerful trump card when overwhelmed.

Enemy variety is impressive, with mythological creatures both old and new making appearances. Cyclops, harpies, and even some unexpected foes stand between Kratos and his goal. Each enemy type requires different tactics, keeping combat fresh throughout the 12-hour campaign.

Visual and Audio Design

The game is stunning on modern hardware. From the sun-drenched streets of Sparta to the gloomy depths of Tartarus, every environment is richly detailed. Character models are expressive, and the creature design is top-notch. The game runs at a smooth 60fps on PS5 and high-end PCs.

The soundtrack deserves special mention. It mixes classic God of War themes with new compositions that capture the tragedy and rage of Kratos. The voice acting is superb, with the cast delivering performances that add weight to the mythological drama.

Pros and Cons

Pros

  • Visceral, satisfying combat
  • Compelling story about Kratos' past
  • Beautiful graphics and art direction
  • Epic boss battles
  • Great soundtrack

Cons

  • Camera can be problematic in tight spaces
  • Some repetitive enemy types
  • Puzzle sections feel dated
  • Relatively short campaign

Story and Characters

Without spoiling anything, Sons of Sparta adds meaningful depth to Kratos' character. We see the man behind the monster, the pain that drives his rage, and the tragic events that shaped him. Supporting characters, including his brother Deimos and the ghost of Athena, add emotional weight to the journey.

The writing strikes a good balance between the over-the-top violence the series is known for and genuine character moments. It's a reminder that Kratos is more than just an angry demigod – he's a tragic figure cursed by his own actions.

Verdict

God of War: Sons of Sparta is a triumphant return to form that honors the series' legacy while pushing it forward. With its brutal combat, compelling story, and stunning presentation, it's an essential experience for fans of the series and newcomers alike. This is, without question, the best prequel the series could have asked for.
