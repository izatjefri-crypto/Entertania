Getting Started Guide

Day 1: Survive Your First Night

Punch trees for wood (at least 10 logs). Craft a crafting table and wooden pickaxe. Mine 19 stone for stone tools. Build a basic shelter (at least 5x5) with a door before nightfall.

10 min 3 Wood, 19 Cobblestone

First Week: Establish Your Base

Create a farm (wheat, carrots, potatoes). Find coal for torches. Explore caves for iron. Make a bed to skip nights. Set up storage system.

2-3 hours 5+ Iron Ore

The Nether: Your First Dimension

Mine 10 obsidian (use diamond pickaxe). Build a Nether portal (4x5 minimum). Collect blaze rods (for brewing) and nether quartz. Find a nether fortress.

1-2 hours 10 Obsidian

The End: Defeat the Ender Dragon

Find a stronghold using Eyes of Ender. Activate the End Portal. Destroy end crystals first. Shoot the dragon with bows. Place beds to explode (Java edition trick).

2-3 hours Final Boss

Essential Tools Progression

Wooden Tools

First day tools. 2 logs → 8 planks → 4 sticks. Minimal durability.

Stone Tools

Day 1-2. 3 cobblestone + 2 sticks per tool. 2x durability of wood.

Iron Tools

Week 1. 3 iron ingots + 2 sticks. 6x durability of stone.

Diamond Tools

Mid-game. 3 diamonds + 2 sticks. 4x durability of iron.

Netherite Tools

End-game. Upgrade diamond tools with netherite ingots. Lava resistant.

Biomes & World Generation

World Layers

Minecraft worlds generate with distinct layers and biomes. Understanding them helps you find specific resources.

Major Biome Categories

🌲 Forest Biomes

Oak/ birch trees, wolves, rabbits. Good for starter bases.

🏜️ Desert Biomes

Sand, cacti, temples, villages. No wood or water.

⛰️ Mountain Biomes

Emeralds, goats, exposed caves. Great for mining.

🌊 Ocean Biomes

Shipwrecks, ocean monuments, kelp. Requires respiration.

Overworld

• Y levels: -64 to 320
• Villages, temples, mineshafts
• All basic resources
• Day/night cycle

The Nether

• Y levels: 0-128
• Nether fortresses, bastions
• Blaze rods, quartz, ancient debris
• Permanent fire resistance needed

The End

• Floating islands
• End cities, ships
• Chorus fruit, elytra, shulkers
• Void below - don't fall!

Structures & Puzzles

Desert Temple

Found in deserts. Contains 4 chests of loot in the basement.

⚠️ TRAP: Center block has pressure plate connected to TNT. Dig around carefully!

Jungle Temple

Two puzzle rooms with levers and dispensers.

Solution: Pull levers in correct order (3 levers on wall). Then place items in dispensers to open door.

Ocean Monument

Guardian-infested structure with elder guardians.

Solution: Mine through with water breathing. Kill 3 elder guardians for mining fatigue removal. Find 8 gold blocks hidden in structure.

Woodland Mansion

Huge structure in dark forests with various rooms.

Rooms: Lava room (hidden chest), fake end portal, illusioner room, vindicator spawners. Bring blocks to navigate.

Stronghold

Underground structure leading to The End.

End Portal: Fill 12 end portal frames with eyes of ender. Silverfish spawners throughout. Libraries have good loot.

Ancient City

Deep dark biome structure (Y=-51). Warden spawns here.

⚠️ DANGER: Use wool to muffle vibrations. Sneak everywhere. Don't trigger sculk shriekers. Chests contain swift sneak enchantment.

Tips, Tricks & Redstone

Village Trading

Cure zombie villagers for discounts. Build trading halls for emeralds. Librarians give mending books.

Diamond Mining

Mine at Y=-59 with branch mining (2x1 tunnels, 3 blocks apart). Use fortune III for more diamonds.

Infinite Water

Place 2 water source blocks with 1 block gap → infinite water pool. Essential for farming.

Lava Farming

Use cauldrons in the Nether. Lava fills cauldrons, renewable fuel source.

XP Farms

Spawner farms (dungeons) or enderman farms (End). Best XP sources in the game.

Shield Use

Right-click to block. Blocks 100% damage from front. Use with axe to disable enemy shields.

Redstone Basics

Redstone Dust

Transmits power up to 15 blocks. Use repeaters to extend.

Repeater

Delays signal, extends range, locks with another repeater.

Comparator

Compares signals, reads containers, subtract mode.
