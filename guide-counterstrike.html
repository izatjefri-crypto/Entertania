Essential Settings

Sensitivity

Pro average: 800 DPI, 1.0-1.5 in-game. Find what's comfortable.

Crosshair

Style 1 or 2, static recommended. Use crashz' crosshair map.

Video Settings

4:3 stretched (1280x960) or 16:9 native. All low for FPS.

Audio

Sound eq: 0. Enable HRTF for better positional audio.

Beginner Tips

Crosshair Placement

Always aim at head level. Never look at the ground while moving.

Counter-Strafing

Tap opposite direction key to stop instantly for accurate shots.

Communication

Give clear, concise callouts. "2 A long" not "they're over there".

Learn Callouts

Study map callouts before playing competitive.

Weapons & Utility Guide

Rifles (CT/T)

M4A4 / M4A1-S

CT standard rifles. M4A1-S for stealth, M4A4 for fire rate.

AK-47

T error. One-tap headshots. Recoil pattern: 7, 1, 5, 10, etc.

AWP

$4750. One-shot kill to body. Slow movement, quick scoping.

Budget rifles. $2050/$1800. Good for force buys.

Economy Management

Buy Rounds

Full Buy ($3700+)

Rifle + armor + utility. Usually after winning 2 rounds.

Force Buy ($2000-3000)

SMGs/pistols + armor. 2nd round after loss.

Eco Round ($0-1500)

Save money. Pistols only. Try to plant for extra cash.

Half-Buy ($2000-3500)

FAMAS/Galil + armor. Limited utility.

Loss Bonus System

Round 1 loss: $1900

First loss bonus. Usually eco or light buy.

Round 2 loss: $2400

Second consecutive loss.

Round 3 loss: $2900

Third consecutive loss.

Round 4+ loss: $3400

Maximum loss bonus.

Win Bonus

$3250 for winning a round. Plant bonus: $800 for T side plant.

Pro Tips & Advanced Mechanics

Peeking

Use shoulder peek to bait shots. Wide swing when you have info.

Pre-fire

Learn common angles and pre-fire them as you peek.

Jiggle Peek

Quick A-D movement to gather info without committing.

Sound Cues

Learn footstep distances. Shift-walk when near enemies.

Practice Routines

Daily Warmup (15 min)

Deathmatch (10 min) + Aim Botz/Recoil Master (5 min) + Retake servers.

Utility Usage

Pop Flashes

Flash off walls so it pops just as you peek. Practice in workshop maps.
