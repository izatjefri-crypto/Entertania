Hey man, I feel your pain! Malenia took me a week to beat. For waterfowl dance in phase 2, the trick is to run away for the first flurry, then roll INTO the second flurry (towards her), then roll backwards for the third. Also, frost pots can stagger her out of the animation if you're quick enough!

Your build looks solid, but maybe try adding some frost affinity to one of your greatswords? Frostbite procs help a lot.

Bloodhound Step is good but you need to time it perfectly. For waterfowl dance, try this:

1. First flurry: BHS twice away from her
2. Second flurry: BHS directly towards her (you'll i-frame through it)
3. Third flurry: BHS backwards

Also, consider using the Blasphemous Blade for phase 2. The weapon art does massive damage and heals you. And don't forget to summon Tiche if you're really stuck!
