Getting Started

Leon vs Claire: Who Should You Pick?

Your choice affects the story, weapons, and certain areas you can access. For your first playthrough, either character works, but here's what to expect:

Leon S. Kennedy

Rookie cop on his first day. Starts with the Matilda handgun. Focuses on shotgun and magnum weapons. Story focuses on Ada Wong and the search for survivors.

👮‍♂️ Rookie Cop

Claire Redfield

College student looking for her brother. Starts with the SLS 60 revolver. Focuses on grenade launcher and SMG weapons. Story focuses on Sherry Birkin.

👧 College Student

Difficulty Levels

Assisted

Enemies deal less damage, auto-heal, infinite saves. Great for beginners focusing on story.

Standard

Balanced difficulty. Unlimited saves in safe rooms. Recommended for first playthrough.

Hardcore

Enemies hit harder, limited saves with Ink Ribbons. Typewriter saves only. True survival horror experience.

Essential Early Tips

Save Ink Ribbons

On Hardcore mode, Ink Ribbons are limited. Only save after major progress. On Standard, save freely in safe rooms.

Combine Herbs

Green + Green = better healing. Green + Red = full heal. Green + Blue = antidote. Red + Blue + Green = ultimate recovery.

Shoot for Knees

Aiming for zombie legs can make them stumble, giving you time to run past. Save ammo by avoiding unnecessary kills.

Explore Everything

Check every corner for gunpowder, herbs, and ammo. Many important items are hidden in desks and lockers.

Mr. X (The Tyrant)

After the helicopter crashes into the police station, Mr. X will begin stalking you. He cannot be killed, only slowed down. Learn to avoid him by walking, not running, and hiding in safe rooms [citation:8].

Boss Strategies

G (William Birkin) Phases

G Phase 1 (Underground Facility)

Leon: Shoot the glowing eye. Use grenades to stagger. Stay near walls to avoid grabs.

Claire: Acid rounds do massive damage. Aim for the eye.

G Phase 2 (Sewers)

Shoot the eye on his shoulder. When he charges, dodge to the side. Use explosive weapons on the containers above to drop on him.

G Phase 3 (Laboratory)

Stay on the outer edge. Shoot eye when it opens. Use the pillars for cover. Save magnum/grenade ammo for this fight.

Other Bosses

Giant Alligator

Leon Only: Run and avoid debris. Shoot the red tank in its mouth when it opens wide. One shot with any weapon works.

Tip: Wait until the mouth is fully open before shooting

Super Tyrant

Final boss for Leon. Shoot him continuously. When he does his charge attack, dodge. Use the rocket launcher when it drops for instant kill.

Save all heavy ammo for this fight

Mr. X Survival Tips

• Walk instead of run when he's nearby (running makes noise)
• Safe rooms are completely safe - he cannot enter
• If he spots you, lead him away then double back
• Flash grenades stun him for several seconds
• He can be slowed down with enough damage, but not killed

Tips & Secrets

Beginner Tips

Zombie Management

Don't kill every zombie. It's often better to shoot their legs to stagger and run past. Ammo conservation is key.

Herb Combinations

G+G = stronger heal | G+R = full heal | G+B = antidote | R+G+B = ultimate defense boost

Gunpowder Recipes

Gunpowder (Large) + Gunpowder = Handgun Ammo | Gunpowder (Large) + High-Grade = Shotgun/SMG Ammo

Explore Thoroughly

Always check the map for red rooms (unexplored) and blue rooms (all items found).

2nd Run Secrets [citation:1]

After completing the game once, you unlock "2nd Run" mode with the other character. This features:

• Different starting location in the police station
• Already unlocked shortcuts from the first playthrough
• New puzzle solutions
• The true ending of the game

Hidden Room: STARS Office Armory

Find the STARS Badge in the Interrogation Room jewelry box. Examine it to flip it over, revealing a USB port. Use it on the laptop in the STARS Office to unlock the armory containing the Lightning Hawk (Leon) or MQ 11 (Claire) [citation:8].

⚠️ HARDCOURSE MODE: On Hardcore, saves require Ink Ribbons (limited), enemies deal 200% damage, and there are no auto-saves. Plan your route carefully and save before major boss fights [citation:1].
