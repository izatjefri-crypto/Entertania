Cyberpunk 2077: Phantom Liberty Review

Reviewed: September 26, 2023
By DAN STAPLETON
Posted: 3 weeks ago
20 comments
9.0 Amazing

The redemption arc is complete

Phantom Liberty isn't just a great expansion—it's the version of Cyberpunk 2077 we were promised. With a gripping spy-thriller narrative, Idris Elba's charismatic performance, and meaningful improvements to core systems, this is Night City at its absolute best.

— Dan Stapleton

Introduction

Three years after its tumultuous launch, Cyberpunk 2077 has finally completed its redemption arc. Phantom Liberty isn't just another expansion—it's a transformative experience that elevates the base game to the heights we always knew it could reach. With a new district, a compelling spy-thriller narrative, and Idris Elba in a career-defining voice role, this is CD Projekt Red at their storytelling best.

Set in the brand-new Dogtown district, Phantom Liberty casts you as V, once again voiced by the excellent Cherami Leigh or Gavin Drea, who gets entangled in a high-stakes espionage plot involving the FIA and a mysterious sleeper agent. What follows is a tense, twisty narrative that rivals the best spy fiction.

"Phantom Liberty isn't just a great expansion—it's the version of Cyberpunk 2077 we were promised three years ago. It's a spy-thriller masterpiece."

Welcome to Dogtown

The new Dogtown district is a masterclass in environmental storytelling. Once a luxurious resort district, now a militarized zone controlled by the ruthless Kurt Hansen, every corner tells a story. Crumbling luxury hotels stand alongside makeshift markets, and the constant threat of the Barghest militia keeps you on edge. It's dense, vertical, and packed with secrets.

The district feels distinct from the rest of Night City—grittier, more dangerous, and more personal. Side jobs and gigs in Dogtown are among the best in the game, each telling a mini-story that adds depth to this war-torn corner of the city.

Idris Elba as Solomon Reed

Idris Elba's performance as Solomon Reed is nothing short of phenomenal. He brings a gravitas and vulnerability to the role that anchors the entire expansion. Reed is a FIA sleeper agent who's been undercover for years, and Elba captures the weariness of a man who's lost his sense of self. His chemistry with V is electric, and their scenes together are some of the most memorable in the entire game.

He's joined by Christine Minji Chang as Song So Mi, a brilliant netrunner with her own secrets, and the always-excellent Keanu Reeves returns as Johnny Silverhand, who has plenty of commentary on the spy games unfolding around you.

Gameplay Improvements

Phantom Liberty isn't just a new story—it comes with a massive 2.0 update that overhauls almost every system in the base game. The perk system has been completely redesigned, vehicle combat has been added, and the police system is finally what we wanted at launch. These improvements make the entire game better, whether you're playing the expansion or not.

The new relic skill tree adds cyberware abilities that let you do things like dash mid-air, slow time, or unleash devastating chain attacks. It adds a new layer of build-crafting that veteran players will love.

Pros and Cons

Pros

  • Gripping spy-thriller narrative
  • Idris Elba's outstanding performance
  • Dogtown is a fantastic new district
  • Gameplay 2.0 overhaul transforms the experience
  • Multiple endings with real consequences
  • Excellent side content and gigs

Cons

  • Some performance issues persist on last-gen (but Phantom Liberty is current-gen only)
  • Main story is relatively short (15-20 hours)
  • Requires the 2.0 update which changes many base game systems

Visual and Audio Design

Phantom Liberty is stunning. Dogtown's unique aesthetic—a blend of war-torn luxury and desperate makeshift living—is rendered in gorgeous detail. Ray tracing effects, especially at night, create some of the most atmospheric moments in any game. Character models are incredibly detailed, and the new weapons and vehicles look fantastic.

The soundtrack by P.T. Adamczyk and Jacek Paciorkowski is another highlight, blending tense spy-thriller music with the industrial cyberpunk sound we love. The licensed tracks, including a new song by Idris Elba himself, are perfectly placed.

Multiple Endings

Without spoiling anything, Phantom Liberty introduces a new ending path for the base game that is both heartbreaking and thematically perfect. The choices you make in the expansion have real weight, and the conclusion had me staring at the credits in silence. It's CD Projekt Red at their narrative best.

Verdict

Cyberpunk 2077: Phantom Liberty is the redemption story that CD Projekt Red needed. It's a masterfully crafted spy-thriller that stands alongside the best expansions in gaming history. Combined with the 2.0 update, it transforms Cyberpunk 2077 into the game we always hoped it would be. For fans of the genre, or anyone who's ever been curious about Night City, this is essential. An easy 9/10.
