Getting Started Guide

Getting Started: The Basics

ARC Raiders is a free-to-play third-person PvE shooter. You play as a Raider fighting against ARC, an invading robotic force.

Beginner Guide

Your First Mission: Welcome to the Resistance

Complete the tutorial missions to learn basic movement, shooting mechanics, and crafting system.

30 min

Essential Weapons

Assault Rifle

Starter weapon. Balanced damage and fire rate.

Bobcat SMG

The Bobcat is a fully automatic submachine gun with high fire rate but low accuracy. Uses Light Ammo.

Vita Spray

A medical item that continuously restores health during use. Can be used on yourself or your allies.

Beginner Tips

Stick Together

ARC Raiders is team-based. Stay close to your squad.

Use Cover

Enemies deal heavy damage. Always use cover.

Upgrade Often

Collect resources to upgrade weapons between missions.

Weapons & Items

Weapons, or guns, are projectile- or energy-based implements that are used to inflict damage, destroy, or cause harm. They are primarily used by Raiders to attack ARC or other Raiders.

Weapon Classes

Assault Rifles

Balanced handling and shooting capabilities. Versatile for all situations.

Shotguns

High close-range damage. Effective against armored ARC units.

SMGs

High fire rate, low recoil. Perfect for quick engagements.

Pistols

versatile, lightweight sidearms designed for close-to-mid-range combat.

Hand Cannons

a specific category of high-damage weapons designed for heavy, precise damage against armored ARC enemies and for bursting down player shields.

Sniper Rifles

high-damage, long-range weapons primarily used for picking off targets.

LMGs

A single, distinct weapon: the Torrente. It is designed for sustained fire and suppression rather than stealth or high mobility. .

Weapon Rarity & Upgrades

Each weapon is loaded with a single type of ammo that dictates how well it penetrates ARC armor. Most weapons can be upgraded, or leveled up, at the Gunsmith to permanently improve how well they handle.

Level I - Basic Level II - Improved Level III - Advanced Level IV - Master

Legendary rarity weapons cannot be upgraded.

ARC Raiders Weapons

Rattler

The Rattler is a fully-automatic Assault Rifle that uses Medium Ammo. It is an inexpensive but solid weapon that offers good damage output and accuracy at close- to medium-range.

Arpeggio

The Arpeggio is an Assault Rifle that uses Medium Ammo. It shoots three bullets per pull of the trigger.

Tempest

The Tempest is a fully-automatic Assault Rifle that uses Medium Ammo.

Bettina

The Bettina is a fully-automatic Assault Rifle that uses Heavy Ammo.

Ferro

The Ferro is a break-action Battle Rifle that uses Heavy Ammo. It is an inexpensive but effective option for taking engagements at medium- to long-range, but its lengthy reload speed can make it a liability when fights get close.

Renegade

The Renegade is a lever-action Battle Rifle that uses Medium Ammo. When fully upgraded, it is a powerful weapon in the hands of a skilled marksman.

Aphelion

The Aphelion is a Battle Rifle that uses Energy Clips to fire energy-based projectiles. It cannot be upgraded.

Stitcher

The Stitcher is a fully-automatic Submachine Gun (SMG) that uses Light Ammo.

Bobcat

The Bobcat is a fully-automatic Submachine Gun (SMG) that uses Light Ammo.

Il Toro

The Il Toro is a pump-action Shotgun that uses Shotgun Ammo. Its high damage output makes it capable of downing most Raiders at close range with only two or three body shots. Ammo is reloaded one-by-one.

Vulcano

The Vulcano is a semi-automatic Shotgun that uses Shotgun Ammo.

Hairpin

The Hairpin is a single-action Pistol that uses Light Ammo. It has a built-in silencer.

Burletta

The Burletta is a semi-automatic Pistol that uses Light Ammo.

Venator

The Venator is a semi-automatic Pistol that uses Medium Ammo. It is a fairly fast-firing pistol that shoots two projectiles at a time. It is particularly effective at close- to medium-range combat. Despite firing two bullets per shot, only one unit of ammo is removed from the magazine when the trigger is pulled.

Anvil

The Anvil is a single-action Hand Cannon that uses Heavy Ammo. It is a versatile hand cannon that excels at hitting targets at medium-range, but its slow firing speed can make the Anvil feel clunky at close-range. Raiders who choose this weapon will often be rewarded if their aim is true.

Torrente

The Torrente is a fully-automatic Light Machine Gun (LMGs) that uses Medium Ammo.

Osprey

The Osprey is a bolt-action Sniper Rifle that uses Medium Ammo. It excels at hitting long-range targets with the help of its scope.

Jupiter

The Jupiter is a bolt-action Sniper Rifle that uses Energy Clips. It cannot be upgraded.

Weapon Mods

Weapon mods, also known as modifications or attachments, are items that are used to adjust weapon properties. Each weapon has a limited number of special slots that can accept mods. Unlike upgrades, weapon mods can be freely added and removed.

It's recommended for Raiders to try different weapon mods in the Practice Range to learn what combinations work best.

Scopes

Red dot, holographic, 2x, 4x, 8x magnification options.

Barrels

Suppressors, compensators, extended barrels.

Magazines

Extended mags, quick reload, drum mags.

Grips

Vertical grip, angled grip, laser sight.

Ammo Types

Standard Rounds

Basic ammo. Effective against unarmored targets.

Armor-Piercing

Increased penetration. Best against heavy ARC units.

Energy Cells

Ammo used for energy weapons. One clip will fully charge a single weapon.

Environmental Puzzles & Secrets

Power Relay Puzzles

Found in Industrial Ruins and The Foundry. You need to redirect power to open doors or activate machinery.

Solution: Follow the cables from the power source. Rotate relays to connect the path. Green lights indicate correct connection. Red means blocked.

Security Terminal Hacks

Found in ARC Command zones. Terminals require code breaking to disable security or access loot rooms.

Solution: Match the symbols in the correct sequence. The pattern is shown briefly at the start. Watch carefully!

Laser Grid Navigation

Found in high-security areas. Moving laser grids block paths to valuable loot.

Solution: Look for floor panels that temporarily disable sections. Time your movement between sweeps. Using the Scanner mod reveals safe timing windows.

Resource Cache Puzzles

Hidden caches throughout all zones require simple puzzles to open.

Solution Types:
• Pressure Plates: Stand on all plates simultaneously (use squad)
• Sequence Buttons: Press in correct order (follow wall markings)
• Resonator Puzzles: Match frequency by adjusting dials
• Light Reflectors: Aim your weapon's flashlight at sensors

Emergency Shutdown Sequence

Found in The Foundry boss area. Must shut down safety protocols before the boss fight.

Solution: Three terminals around the arena must be activated within 30 seconds of each other. Coordinate with your squad and communicate!

ARC Raiders Tips & Strategies

Squad Composition

Always run a balanced squad: 1 Assault (Rattler/Tempest), 1 Support (Stitcher/Repair Tool), 1 Sniper (Osprey/Renegade). This covers all combat situations.

Weak Points

All ARC units have glowing weak points. Shoot these for 3x damage. Scout Drones: Power core on back. Brutes: Exposed coolant tanks.

Upgrade Priority

Upgrade your primary weapon to Level II first. Then save for mod slots. Damage upgrades > Fire rate > Reload speed for most weapons.

Healing & Revival

Carry at least 2 Medkits. When reviving teammates, deploy smoke cover first. The Repair Tool can heal from range.

Resource Farming

Best scrap farming: Zone 1 speed runs (5-7 min). Best rare materials: Zone 3 boss runs. Always loot every container.

Enemy Priority

Kill order: Shieldbearers (disable shields) > Grenadiers (explosive) > Stalkers (flanking) > Brutes (last, they're slow).

Boss Strategies

Zone 3 Boss: The Juggernaut

Phase 1: Shoot leg joints to slow it down. Avoid the ground slam attack (red circle indicator).

Phase 2: Back armor opens revealing core. One player distracts while others damage core.

Phase 3: Enraged mode. Use the environment for cover. Save ultimate abilities for this phase.

Zone 4 Boss: ARC Commander

Phase 1: Shielded by drones. Kill drones first (4 total).

Phase 2: Commander attacks with energy blades. Maintain distance, shoot when it overextends.

Phase 3: Calls reinforcements. Focus Commander while support players handle adds.

Pro Tip: Save your best weapon mods and consumables for this fight.

Pro Tips

Ping System

Use the ping system constantly. Mark enemies, loot, and danger. Communication wins games.

Tactical Retreat

If a mission goes bad, retreat to previous area. Enemies won't follow through loading zones.

Daily Missions

Always complete daily missions for bonus credits and rare materials. Resets every 24 hours.

Free Rewards

Check the shop daily for free item bundles. Sometimes contains weapon mods and consumables.
