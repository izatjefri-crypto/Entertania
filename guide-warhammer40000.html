Beginner Tips for Warhammer 40K

Start with Space Marines

Most beginner-friendly faction. Balanced stats, straightforward mechanics, iconic units.

Play Tutorials

Each game has extensive tutorials. Don't skip them - learn cover, abilities, and mechanics.

Join a Community

Warhammer has a huge community. Join Discord servers for tips and group play.

Learn Your Class

Each class has specific roles. Play to your strengths - tank, damage, support.

Building Your First Army (1000pts)

HQ Choice

Start with a Captain or Chapter Master. Provides buffs and melee power.

Troops

2-3 units of Intercessors or Tactical Marines. Objective holders.

Elites

Terminators or Bladeguard. Heavy hitters for mid-board.

Heavy Support

Eradicators or Devastators. Anti-tank firepower.

Strategy & Tactics

Combat Tips

Use Cover

Cover gives +1 save. Ruins block line of sight. Always position models in cover.

Focus Fire

Concentrate fire on one unit at a time. Remove threats completely.

Hold Objectives

Primary scoring wins games. Don't chase kills, hold points.

Synergy

Keep buffing characters near units they support. Auras matter.

Mission Tactics

Deployment Zones

Hammer and Anvil, Dawn of War, Search and Destroy. Adapt strategy to deployment.

Secondary Objectives

Choose secondaries that match your army. Engage on All Fronts, Assassination, etc.

Beginner Mistakes to Avoid

• Don't overextend your HQ early

• Always screen your backline

• Remember psychic phase abilities

• Don't forget command point rerolls

• Measure charges before committing
