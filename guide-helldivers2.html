Getting Started as a Helldiver

Core Concepts

You fight for Super Earth. Complete missions to liberate planets and earn rewards.

Patriotic

Co-op

Critical

Live Service

Beginner Tips for New Recruits

Stick Together

Never wander alone. Strength in numbers. 4 Helldivers are a force of nature.

Watch Your Fire

Friendly fire is permanent. Don't shoot your teammates. Call out stratagems.

Reinforce Smart

Throw reinforcement beacons to safe locations. Don't drop teammates into enemy swarms.

Complete Objectives

Main objectives first, then side objectives. Extract is optional but gives bonus XP.

Enemy Factions & Weaknesses

Terminids (Bugs)

Swarm enemies. Melee focused. Weak to fire and explosives.

Swarm Melee

Automaton (Bots)

Mechanical enemies. Heavy armor, ranged attacks. Weak to anti-armor.

Armored Ranged

Illuminate (Squids)

Returning faction. Advanced tech, shields. Weak to energy weapons.

Shields Advanced

Terminid Threat Levels

Scavenger / Warrior

Basic melee units. Headshots with primary weapons. Swarm in groups.

Hive Guard

Armored front. Shoot rear or use explosives. Flank with team.

Charger

Heavy armor. Shoot leg armor off, then exposed flesh. Recoilless Rifle one-shot.

Titan

Massive bio-form. Orbital strikes, 500kg bomb, or multiple Recoilless shots.

Automaton Threat Levels

Trooper / Commissar

Basic infantry. Headshots with primary. Commissar buffs nearby bots.

Devastator

Heavy infantry. Rocket or minigun variants. Aim for head or use anti-material rifle.

Tank

Heavy armor vehicle. Orbital lasers, Eagle strikes, or Recoilless to rear.

Factory Strider

Walking fortress. Multiple cannons. Needs coordinated team fire.

Extraction Tips

• Call extract early. Hold position for 2 minutes.

• Use smoke strikes for cover.

• Clear surrounding area before extract lands.

• Last diver to board - cover your team.

Advanced Tips & Tactics

Combat Tactics

Kiting

Run, turn, shoot. Never stop moving vs bugs. Use terrain to break line of sight.

Focus Fire

Coordinate with team. Take down one heavy at a time. Spread damage is wasted.

Grenade Cooking

Hold grenade to cook. Time explosion for mid-air detonation.

Dive Mechanics

Dive to avoid explosions. Dive while stimming for animation cancel.

Team Composition

Anti-Tank Specialist

Recoilless Rifle, EAT-17, Orbital Railcannon. Hunts heavies.

Support / Utility

Shield Generator, Supply Pack, Stun Grenades. Keeps team alive.

Area Denial

Mortar Turret, Orbital Napalm, Eagle Cluster. Clears chaff.
