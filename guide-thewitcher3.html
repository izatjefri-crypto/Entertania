Getting Started

Game Modes Explained [citation:1]

Default mode. Start from the beginning with Lv1 Geralt. Best for first-time players.

Difficulty Levels

Just the Story

Easy difficulty. Focus on narrative. Enemies deal less damage.

Story and Sword

Normal difficulty. Balanced combat. Recommended for first playthrough.

Blood and Broken Bones

Hard difficulty. Enemies hit harder. Better loot drops.

Death March

Hardest difficulty. Enemies have more health. Trophy for completion.

Essential Early Tips [citation:3][citation:6]

Explore Everything

Check notice boards for contracts and side quests. Discover ? marks on map for loot and XP.

Eat Food

Always carry food for health regeneration. Bread, water, and meat are essential early game.

Practice Dodging

Learn to dodge and roll. Blocking doesn't work against monsters. Timing is everything.

Loot Everything

Loot all containers but avoid guards in cities. Sell junk to merchants for coin.

Main Story Walkthrough

Prologue: Kaer Morhen

Tutorial section. Learn combat basics, follow Vesemir, fight ghouls, find Vesemir's crystal skull. Choose to run the walls for movement tutorial. [citation:8]

30 min 250 XP

The Nilfgaardian Connection

Travel to White Orchard, meet the innkeeper, learn Gwent basics, interrogate patrons. First introduction to the world.

45 min Gwent Cards

Wandering in the Dark [citation:5]

Follow Keira Metz into elven ruins. Navigate through portals, fight drowners and rats, destroy rat nests with Igni. Defeat the golem and Nithral of the Wild Hunt.

90 min 650+ XP Eye of Nehaleni

The Battle of Kaer Morhen [citation:2]

Defend the keep from the Wild Hunt. Close portals using dimeritium bombs or Yrden. Protect Lambert, help Triss fight warriors, retreat to the main gate.

60 min Critical Story Point

Blood on the Battlefield

Aftermath of the battle. Deal with the consequences and prepare for the final confrontation.

45 min Branching Choices

Combat & Signs Guide

Witcher Signs

Quen

Protective shield. Absorbs damage. Essential for Death March difficulty.

Igni

Fire stream. Sets enemies ablaze. Good for destroy nests and crowd control.

Aard

Telekinetic blast. Knocks back enemies. Can destroy walls and stalagmites.

Axii

Mind control. Turns enemies into allies. Opens dialogue options.

Yrden

Magic trap. Slows enemies. Essential for wraiths and closing portals.

Combat Tips

Dodge vs Roll

Dodge for short movement to stay close. Roll to create distance from groups.

Parry Humans

Parry works against humans, not monsters. Time it right for counter attacks.

Target Weakness

Use bestiary to learn enemy weaknesses. Apply correct oils and signs.

Upgrade Signs

Invest skill points in sign upgrades. Alternate sign modes are powerful.

Best Early Game Skills [citation:4]

Gourmet

Food regeneration lasts 20 minutes. Best early game survivability skill.

Exploding Shield

Quen shield knocks back enemies when broken. Great for crowd control.

Strength Training

Increases attack power with swords. Core damage skill.

Crafting & Alchemy

Witcher Gear Schools [citation:6][citation:9]

Ursine School

Heavy armor. High protection, slow stamina regen. Found in Skellige.

Feline School

Light armor. High attack power, critical hits. Found in Novigrad/Velen.

Griffin School

Medium armor. Sign intensity boost. Found in Velen.

Wolven School

Balanced stats. Added in Blood and Wine DLC.

Essential Potions [citation:3]

Swallow

Basic healing potion. Regenerates vitality over time.

Thunderbolt

Increases attack power. Best for boss fights.

Maribor Forest

Increases adrenaline gain. Helps with sign casting.

White Raffard's Decoction

Instant healing. Emergency potion.

Alchemy Tips

Toxicity Management

Max toxicity increases with alchemy skills. Use decoctions wisely - they last longer but use more toxicity.

Meditation Refills

Meditating with any alcohol in inventory refills all potions, bombs, and blade oils. Strong alcohol works best.

Tips, Tricks & Secrets

Money Making Guide [citation:4]

Loot and Sell

Loot everything, sell weapons to blacksmiths, armor to armorers. Best prices in Novigrad.

? Marks

Clear all question marks on map. Hidden treasures often contain valuable gear.

Gwent Cards

Play Gwent early, buy all cards from innkeepers. Some cards are missable.

Witcher Contracts

Always negotiate higher pay. Complete contracts for best coin and XP.

Gwent Guide [citation:4]

Gwent: Playing Thaler

Complete "A Deadly Plot" then find Thaler at the Seven Cats Inn. He plays Scoia'tael deck with Muster units. Use Biting Frost to counter. [citation:4]

Missable Cards

Some Gwent cards are missable. Play everyone, buy all cards, complete all Gwent quests before finishing the game.

Place of Power Locations [citation:4]

Places of Power grant one free ability point when activated. Found throughout all regions. Use them early for skill boosts.

DLC Expansions [citation:7]

Follows Olgierd von Everec and Gaunter O'Dimm. Introduces Shani. Recommended Level 30+.

Blood and Wine

Travel to Toussaint. New region, new mutations, new Gwent cards. Recommended Level 35+.
