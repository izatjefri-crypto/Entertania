Main Story Walkthrough

Chapter 1: The Village Awakens

Grace Ashcroft arrives at the remote Eastern European village after receiving a distress signal. Explore the abandoned houses, learn basic controls, and find your first weapon.

45 min 3 Collectibles

Chapter 2: Leon's Return

Switch to Leon S. Kennedy as he investigates a separate incident connected to the village. Face your first major enemy encounter and discover the underground facility.

60 min 5 Collectibles 1 Boss Fight

Chapter 3: The Old Church

Grace investigates the village church where strange rituals have taken place. Solve the altar puzzle to access the catacombs below.

40 min 2 Collectibles 1 Puzzle

Chapter 4: Underground Laboratory

Both characters' paths converge in the secret Umbrella laboratory. Find keycards, avoid security systems, and discover the truth about the outbreak.

75 min 6 Collectibles 2 Boss Fights

Chapter 5: The Final Experiment

Confront the mastermind behind the outbreak. Prepare for the ultimate battle and choose between multiple endings based on your actions.

50 min 2 Collectibles Final Boss

Puzzle Solutions

Church Altar Puzzle (Chapter 3)

You need to place 3 emblems in the correct order: Lion, Eagle, Snake.

Solution: Lion (left), Eagle (center), Snake (right)

Laboratory Security Lock (Chapter 4)

Find the 3 keycards with color codes: Red, Blue, Green.

Solution: Insert in order found: Blue → Red → Green

Clock Tower Puzzle (Chapter 4)

Set the clock to the time shown in the note (3:15).

Solution: Hour hand to 3, Minute hand to 15

Tips & Tricks

Save Often

Use typewriters whenever you see them. Ink ribbons are plentiful, so don't hoard them.

Parry Mastery

Time your knife parry just as an enemy attacks to stagger them and create an opening.

Explore Everything

Hidden rooms often contain weapon parts and collectibles. Check your map for unexplored areas.

Green Herbs

Mix Green+Green for better healing. Green+Red creates a powerful healing item.

Aim for Heads

Headshots deal extra damage and can stagger enemies. Save ammo by aiming carefully.

Know When to Run

Not every enemy needs to be killed. Save resources by avoiding unnecessary fights.

Boss Strategies

Final Boss Strategy

Phase 1: Shoot the glowing weak point. Phase 2: Use the environment to stun the boss. Phase 3: Switch to magnum and unload all shots. Save your rocket launcher for the final phase.
